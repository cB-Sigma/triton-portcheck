# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and do not harass
anyone. This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other space where the project is
represented.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here makes participation hard for you or others, contact the maintainers privately through
the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/cb-Sigma/triton-portcheck/security/advisories/new) and
say that it is a conduct report. Reports are read by the maintainers only and are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate
these expectations, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
